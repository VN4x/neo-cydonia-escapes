# Neo-Cydonia Escapes Landing Page

## Direction
Build one cinematic scrolling homepage using **Observatory Glass** as the visual and structural foundation. Fold the strongest travel information and dashboard details from the other two concepts into later sections rather than creating separate pages.

Locked visual choices:
- Martian terracotta and rust-red atmosphere
- Blue-shaded cosmos black
- Whitish vanilla interface surfaces and text
- Sparse gold and cyan status signals
- No neon red or neon green
- Syne display typography with Plus Jakarta Sans body text
- Premium orbital-dashboard composition with restrained glass effects

## Homepage
1. **Cinematic opening scene**
   - Generate a full-bleed, first-person Martian vista with terraformed copper-violet haze, a massive realistic Earth or Venus, and Phobos inside an illuminated Saturn-like LED ringscreen.
   - Keep the scene dominant in the opening viewport while revealing the beginning of the next section.
   - Add a thin crystalline header with the Neo-Cydonia mark, live route-status ticker, and Connect Wallet control.
   - Present “NEO-CYDONIA ESCAPES” as the primary visual signal.

2. **Booking console**
   - Center a sharp frosted-glass console over the landscape.
   - Include functional selectors for astro-cycle and gravity preference.
   - Add the single primary action, “LAUNCH ITINERARY,” with clear selected and confirmation states.
   - Adapt gracefully to the current mobile/tablet preview without covering the celestial composition.

3. **Auspicious alignments**
   - Present the Sirian Heliacal Awakening, Venusian Morning Star Conjunction, and Galactic Gate Overlap.
   - Pair each with its vibe, ideal activity, destination, and transit option.
   - Generate three cohesive cinematic destination images matching the opening scene.

4. **Habitat booking band**
   - Show the three supplied premium habitats, cosmic tuning, Crypto-Credit rates, and included perks.
   - Use the denser terminal information style from the alternate concepts while preserving the Observatory Glass design language.

5. **Cosmic timeline preferences**
   - Add compact controls for leisure archetype, group size, and gravity choice.
   - Keep this as part of the same booking experience, not a separate page.

6. **Motion and interaction**
   - Animate the ringscreen ticker, timestamps, glyphs, destination names, and atmospheric light subtly.
   - Add restrained selector, card, and button feedback.
   - Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep readability ahead of spectacle.

## Technical details
- Replace the placeholder homepage and define page-specific title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata.
- Add the selected font links through the document head and carry the chosen palette into semantic design tokens.
- Use generated local imagery rather than external stock images or placeholders.
- Keep all controls keyboard accessible with visible focus states and sufficient contrast.
- Verify the final page at desktop and mobile sizes, including layout stability, animation behavior, and working form interactions.
